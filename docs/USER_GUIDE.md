# User guide - ClockWise

## Add the widget
Long-press an empty spot on the home screen -> Widgets -> ClockWise -> drag it. Or open the app and press **Add the widget to the home screen**. Resize it by long-pressing and dragging the handles: bigger widgets show more (weather, date line, alarm, events, extra clocks, sunrise/sunset).

## Widget types
In the launcher's widget picker you will find: **Full dashboard**, **Clock**, **Weather**, **Agenda and alarm**, and **Slim strip**. Add as many as you like, in any size; all follow the Settings below. Settings -> Look -> "Ready-made looks" switches the whole design in one tap.

## The app
**Home** shows a live preview exactly like the widget. Switch the preview size (small / medium / large) and wallpaper (dark / light) to see how it looks, and pick the clock style: flip cards, digital, analog or minimal.

**Settings** (every section is collapsed with a one-line summary):
- **Look**: style, colours, background transparency (default: fully transparent), info-panel opacity, corner radius, clock size, 24-hour, calm animations (no flip), widget language.
- **What to show**: switch each part on or off - clock, weather, max/min, rain/sunny outlook, Gregorian date, Hebrew date, holidays, alarm, today's events, extra clocks, sunrise/sunset, steps.
- **Places**: search a city or use your current location (coarse, optional). Up to 4; the selected one is shown on the widget.
- **More time zones**: up to 3 small clocks under the main clock (e.g. Jerusalem and New York).
- **Alarm**: choose which alarm app's next alarm is shown (any app, SmartRing, Google Clock, Samsung Clock) and how early it appears.
- **Calendar (Google)**: allow read-only access to the device calendar (Google Calendar syncs into it), choose calendars, how often the banner switches between events.
- **Weather and dates**: Fahrenheit, rain threshold, Hebrew date changes at sunset, holidays as in Israel or abroad.

## Weather details
Tap the weather area of the widget (or "Weather details"): current conditions, max/min, rain outlook, sunrise/sunset and an hour-by-hour list (temperature, rain chance, humidity) for three days.

## Several places, hourly charts
With two or more places, Home lists them all; tap one to open its forecast. In the forecast, swipe between places, pick a day, and switch the chart between temperature, rain and humidity.

## Nightfall and candle lighting
The line under the weather shows sunrise, sunset and nightfall; on a Friday or the eve of a holiday it also shows candle lighting (18 minutes before sunset). Switch it off in Settings.

## Map and places around the world
Open the map card (or "Add a place around the world"): search a city or press "Show map" and tap it. You see the weather and local time there, then save it as "my place" (used by the widget) or as a world place (shown on the dashboard).

## Several timers, labels and alerts
Add as many stopwatches and countdowns as you need. Each has a label, Start/Pause, Restart and Cancel. For a countdown choose what happens at the end: a ringtone (alarm, notification or phone ringtone) and/or vibration, in any combination, and press "Test the alert". The widget shows up to three active timers by label.

## Days until an event
Pick a holiday in one tap or any date; the card shows the days left, and the nearest one also shows in the widget.

## Festive and night looks
Settings > What to show: "Festive look on holidays" and "Automatic day and night look".

## Backup and sharing
Settings > Backup and sharing: send your look as a short code, back up all settings as text, and paste a code to apply it.

## Stopwatch and countdown
Two dashboard cards. Start either one and it also appears as a row in the widget (minutes, updated every minute); the dashboard shows live seconds. The countdown rings a notification when it ends (allow notifications when asked).

## Rain alert and quiet hours
Settings > What to show: turn on the rain alert to get one notification shortly before rain starts at your active place. Quiet hours switch off animations and alerts at night.

## Smart content
Turn on "Smart content" in Settings > What to show: the widget and the dashboard then show only what matters now (events when there are any, the alarm when it is near, sun times at useful hours) and the layout adjusts by itself.

## Dashboard
The home screen is a dashboard of cards: the widget preview, date and holidays, weather, your places, the next alarm, today's events, sun times and (optional) steps and world clocks. Tap a card for details; the buttons open the Calendar or alarm app. Change which cards show and their order in Settings > Dashboard.

## Pocket watch metal
With the pocket-watch clock style, choose gold, silver or rose gold under Look.

## Steps
Switch on "Steps today" in Settings and allow physical activity. The widget footer shows 👣 and today's steps, counted from the phone's own step counter (updates about every 15 minutes).

## Permissions
Settings > Permissions lists every optional permission, why it is asked, and lets you allow it or open system settings.

## Updates and sharing
Settings > Updates: check, download, install. Share the app as a link or as the installed APK.

## Privacy
Only two requests leave the phone: weather and city search (Open-Meteo, rounded coordinates). Location, calendar and alarm are read only if you allow it and never sent anywhere. The Hebrew calendar and holidays are computed on the phone.
