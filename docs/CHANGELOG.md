# Changelog

## 0.3.0
- Ornaments: Purim mask no longer keyed out, Star of David on the flag, Hanukkah picture per day (1-8 candles).
- Direct read of upcoming events from the Special Events app (its new read-only provider), with the calendar as fallback.
- Weekly Torah portion and Omer count; Hanukkah ornament per day; Shabbat and Yom Tov mode; bedside clock; UV and wind charts with comfort words and a rain-radar link; timer presets and a phone ringtone picker; TalkBack description, High-contrast and Big-text presets; two-column dashboard on tablets.
- Birthdays, anniversaries and memorials from the Special Events app (through its calendar) in the widget row and a dashboard card.
- Painted holiday ornaments in the widget corner and the dashboard header on the holiday (festive looks).
- Per-widget settings: each placed widget can have its own look and items.
- Emulator UI tests with screenshots; complete documentation set kept honest by repo tests.

## 0.2.0
- Dashboard home screen with animated, customisable cards (widget preview, date and holidays, weather, places, alarm, events, sun and nightfall, steps, world clocks, map) and drill-downs; shortcuts to the calendar and alarm apps.
- Hourly forecast with animated charts (temperature, rain, humidity); several places side by side; map place picker (OpenStreetMap) and places around the world with local time.
- Several labelled stopwatches and countdowns with pause, restart and cancel; per-countdown ringtone and vibration; days-until-event cards; timers show in the widget.
- Widget: tap each part to open the matching app, settings gear, smart content, quiet hours, festive holiday looks, automatic night look, widget status and repair.
- Updates and sharing from inside the app; nightfall and candle lighting; steps counter; rain alert; backup and share-my-look codes; first-run guide; silver and rose-gold pocket watch.

## 0.1.0
- Foundation: native Android app with a transparent, resizable home-screen widget (flip cards, digital, analog, minimal).
- Hebrew + Gregorian date, holidays, weather (Open-Meteo) with max/min and rain/sunny outlook, next alarm, today's calendar events banner, extra time zones, sunrise/sunset.
- App: live widget preview, settings (look, what to show, places, time zones, alarm app, calendar, units), weather details by hour.
